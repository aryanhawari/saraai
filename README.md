## About SARA AI

SARA AI runs on **Aryan Hawari's own AI brain** — a custom engine designed, built and trained by him. No outside AI company powers SARA; every thought comes from his own model.

Aryan is a young developer with one simple dream: to build his own AI. SARA AI is that dream, made real — a fast, friendly and private assistant created by him, for everyone. He designs everything himself: the brain, the thinking, the look. Every update makes SARA smarter, faster and more helpful.

## Inside SARA AI's Brain

- **Own AI Brain** — designed, trained and tuned by Aryan Hawari
- **Real-Time Thinking** — a truthful status begins instantly when you send; verified notes replace fake step narration
- **Ultra Parallel Internet Intelligence** — DuckDuckGo, Mojeek, Wikipedia, Google News, official websites and GitHub search together; Deep Research adds Serper, SerpAPI, Tavily, Brave and Firecrawl when configured
- **Source Verification** — results are deduplicated, ranked by relevance, recency and authority; official sources win conflicts
- **Freshness Guard** — today, latest, current and breaking questions bypass stale cache and disclose if live verification fails
- **Public Social Lookup** — profile URLs and public follower counts cross-checked from public sources; private or logged-in data is never claimed
- **Proactive & Emotional** — Sara senses your mood and picks the right tone
- **Live Voice & Speech** — talk to Sara naturally, in real time
- **Private Memory** — chats and memories stay on your phone, never on a server
